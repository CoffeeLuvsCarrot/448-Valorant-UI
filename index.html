HUD reaction test · Final version

Measures how fast players notice a HUD warning, comparing the current Valorant HUD with our redesign.

Version

To tester:

  1. Do the test in full screen: press F11 on Windows, or Ctrl+Cmd+F on Mac.
  2. Keep your eyes on the crosshair in the middle of the screen.
  3. Small targets will pop up next to the crosshair. Click the mouse to shoot them.
  4. Something on the HUD will change after a few seconds.
  5. Press Space as soon as you notice the change.
  6. Then pick what you noticed (keys 1–5 or click).

Pressing Space too early counts as a false start, and that trial starts again.

After this part, a second part on the spike timer starts with its own rules page.
